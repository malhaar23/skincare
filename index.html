Skincare 101 for Indian skin

A no-fluff starter routine for Indian skin types — built around our climate, common concerns (pigmentation, oiliness, tan), and what's actually available in Indian pharmacies and online.

Skill level: Absolute beginner Budget: Under ₹2,500/month Time: 5 min AM + 5 min PM

The 30-second answer

  • 3 products only. Cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen. That's it for week 1. Don't add anything else until these become habit.
  • SPF is non-negotiable. Indian climate + melanin still tans + UVA causes hyperpigmentation. Daily SPF 30+ is the single biggest result-driver.
  • Skip toner. A marketing invention from the 90s. Modern formulations don't need pH balancing.
  • Don't overdo actives. Indian skin reacts strongly to retinol and AHAs. Start low % twice a week, ramp up slowly.

02The morning routine — 5 minutes

Speed matters more than products. A simple routine you do every day beats an elaborate one you skip half the time.

StepWhatHow long
1Splash with cool water (skip cleanser for AM — your skin was clean overnight)30 sec
2Vitamin C serum (3 drops — optional but powerful for pigmentation)10 sec + 2 min absorb
3Lightweight moisturizer (gel for oily, cream for dry)20 sec
4Sunscreen SPF 30+ (2-finger lengths — most people use 1/4 of what they need)30 sec
5Wait 5 min before makeup/leaving (lets sunscreen set)5 min

03The evening routine — 5 minutes

This is when actives go on. Skin repairs itself overnight, so this is where real progress happens.

StepWhatHow long
1Remove makeup/sunscreen (micellar water or oil cleanser if you wore makeup)1 min
2Cleanse with gentle face wash (gel for oily, cream for dry)30 sec
3Treatment: niacinamide nightly OR retinol 2x/week (only after week 4)30 sec
4Moisturizer (heavier than AM is fine)30 sec
5Lip balm + done10 sec
